| **v87** | 2026-07-31 | **Description line-breaks for real, season-true header art, richer settings.** All three verified against their exact render paths, none guessed: (1) **Descriptions: ratings & synopsis now actually stack.** v86's one-per-line ratings still rendered as one blob — root cause traced into the app's own upstream code: Cloudstream displays descriptions through `HtmlCompat.fromHtml` (`ResultFragmentPhone` → `setTextHtml` → `String.html()`), where raw newlines are whitespace and collapse. It now uses the same v71 absolute window as episode meta (JJK S3 = absolute 48–59), so the header is a real, highest-rated still from inside *your* season — deterministic, no spoilers from other arcs. | **v85** | 2026-07-30 | **bingr-Sirius & mooflix-HDGhar now start like the web: light rung first.** Your report ("streams fine on web, Cloudstream fetches slowly") plus my live measurements told the story: their CDN (the streamraiwind family) serves per-quality ladders — I fetched the real Sirius playlists/segments and they answer fast from anywhere (segment: 200 OK in ~0.1-0.3s regardless of headers), so the CDN was never the bottleneck. The difference was behavior: the web player starts adaptive on a LOW rung and ramps up, while Cloudstream's default pick grabbed the FIRST listed link — the top 8 Mbps 1080p rung — and buffered on it. Both resolvers now collect their per-quality links and emit them LOWEST-quality-first for the Sirius/Bingr servers and Moonflix HDGhar (HV) + the Moonflix mp4 ladder (CH, language order preserved, low-res first inside each language). The default/auto pick now starts light exactly like the web; every rung is still listed with its quality chip, so 1080p is one tap away whenever the network can hold it. Two honest notes: (1) if you *manually* pick the 1080p chip and your ISP can't sustain ~5-8 Mbps to that overseas CDN at that moment, that rung can still buffer — starting low is precisely the fix for that; (2) Sirius sometimes returns nothing for a title (their scrapers are per-title) — that's their catalogue, unchanged. New log lines: `Bingr: emit N link(s) low-first on <server>`, `Moonflix HV: emit N link(s) low-first`, `Moonflix CH: ladder N link(s) low-first`. Carries v84 (sync-line removal, AniList episode titles, franchise cache/hardening) and v83 (no pre-playback verifier). Not pushed to GitHub — your call when to push. |
| **v84** | 2026-07-30 | **Three of your four asks (Sirius/HDghar needs your site details — see chat).** (1) **Sync-status lines removed from descriptions** — the last "👤 AniList: Watching · ep a/b" readout no longer pins itself to anime-page descriptions; the app's own sync sheet under the description still shows every tracker's real numbers. BDIX/link fetching untouched — v83's no-verifier behavior stays. |
| **v83** | 2026-07-30 | **The pre-playback source verifier is gone, at your request.** The v76 liveness probe (the thing that HEAD-checked every link before showing it and hid the ones that didn't answer) is fully removed — links are now listed exactly as the sites give them, zero pre-checks, zero added delay before the list appears.
